Characteristics

PC Library

Wavetables

16 user wavetable slots. Source WAV must be mono PCM16, 32000 Hz, exactly 8192 samples (32 cycles × 256). The MicroFreak uses them with the WaveUser oscillator engine.

Samples

128 sample slots (mono, 32 kHz). Total sample memory: ~3.5 min. Used by the Sample, Scan Grains, Cloud Grains and Hit Grains engines (firmware 5+).

Device Settings

Global settings stored on the MicroFreak. Changes are written immediately and verified with a readback.
